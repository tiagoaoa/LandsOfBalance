## Current sound bank

`tools/build_audio_bank.py` builds `assets/audio/designed/` from those original
recordings and the sources below. Original files remain unchanged. Outputs are
48 kHz, 16-bit mono WAVs for spatial playback, with layered foley, equalization,
pitch edits, trimmed attacks, fades, loop crossfades and peak headroom. Spell
rumble, air movement and electrical beds also contain original procedural layers
generated by the build script.

| Source | Author | License | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Impact Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds) | Kenney | CC0 | Five recorded variations each of grass, concrete and wood footsteps; wood, plate, soft-body and punch impact layers |
| [RPG Sound Pack](https://opengameart.org/content/rpg-sound-pack) | artisticdude | CC0 | Cloth, chainmail, bottle, bubbles, coins, metal ringing, magic and giant vocal layers |

Selected unmodified source files are stored under `assets/audio/source/`, which
is excluded from Godot's import scan. The bank has 36 events and 106 recordings.
The CC-BY / OGA-BY attribution above still applies to derived bow, flesh-hit,
arrow-impact, death, rain and river recordings. Generated assets are not
relicensed as CC0; retain the attribution of their source layers.
