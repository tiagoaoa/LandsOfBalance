The cliff card is an offline render of the existing Poly Haven mountainside scan (CC0): https://polyhaven.com/a/mountainside

The ruin card is an offline render of this project's authored sanctuary kit, textured with the existing Poly Haven castle wall scan (CC0): https://polyhaven.com/a/castle_wall_varriation

Rebuild both images and their dimensions with `tools/art/arena_assets.py` in Blender. The source meshes remain in `assets/environment/mountainside` and `assets/environment/sanctuary`.
