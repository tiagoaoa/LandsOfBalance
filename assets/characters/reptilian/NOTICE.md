Source supplied as `reptillian_alien.glb` in the project root. Attribution and
license are recorded in that file's embedded asset metadata.

Lands of Balance modifications: welded and reduced mesh, combat skeleton and
weights, scale UVs, dark scale albedo/normal/roughness materials. Build script:
`tools/art/prepare_reptilian.py`. Original source retained unchanged.

`reptilian_rex.glb` retains that body and combat skeleton, with an original
T-rex head, articulated jaw, teeth, and iron helmet covering approximately
70% of the face, with modeled dents and blade scars, damaged fasteners,
oxidation maps, and individually chipped/stained teeth. Added geometry
and iron textures are generated by
`tools/art/trex_helmet.py`; editable source is
`tools/art/source/reptilian_rex.blend`. The supplied alien head is removed.
The original source attribution and CC-BY-4.0 license still apply to the body.

`reptilian_arena.glb` is the arena rendering variant of `reptilian_rex.glb`.
`tools/art/arena_assets.py` removes geometry below Blender Z=-3 and caps the cut,
retaining all 21 bones and the existing head, armor, materials and attribution.
